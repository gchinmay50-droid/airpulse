AQI is computed per station from CPCB sub-indices using the National AQI rules: at least 3 pollutants including PM2.5 or PM10, worst sub-index wins. The “16 h rule” is CPCB’s requirement of 16 of the last 24 hours of data; “unknown” means the station has not been observed for a full 24 h window yet. Distance labels: under 5 km representative, 5–25 km indicative, beyond 25 km not covered. A ⚠ marks a pollutant whose sub-index swung more than 150 points within 2 hours - implausible for real air chemistry, more likely a sensor fault.
