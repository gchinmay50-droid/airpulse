Feed status

Every figure here is computed by Apache Flink from the raw stream, not by the feed. CPCB publishes no quality flags; these are the checks a public AQI number should have had before it was shown to anyone.

Dark stations

No reading for 6+ hours, measured against the feed's own latest hour. A dark station still appears in most apps with its last number, however old. AirPulse drops it from "near me".

Stuck sensors

Identical sub-index for 5+ consecutive hours at a value of 20 or more. Below 20, integer rounding of a low concentration flatlines legitimately (measured: 74% of all identical runs). A frozen PM2.5 at 181 feeds a false "Poor" to every app in the city. Emitted one hour behind: an event-time window waits for the watermark.

Implausible jumps

A sub-index swing of more than 150 points between consecutive readings no more than 2 hours apart — two or three CPCB categories in an hour. Real air rarely does that; sensors and feed transcription do.

Stuck sensors: Flink event-time OVER window, 6 h RANGE frame. Jumps: LAG over each (station, pollutant) partition. Dark stations: query-time absence check against the gold table. Window for jumps: last 24 h.
